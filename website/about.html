About ODELHUB Pay

ODELHUB Pay’s public product shell is a tuition & school fees explorer beta: labelled demo schools, student/school stubs, and honesty notices. It does not settle money.

The full Next.js application under ODEL Hub OS/ODELHUB-Pay is a separate surface (typically :3000). ODEI Pay on :5215 is a different merchant OS product.

Local vs proposed host Local: http://localhost:5231/. Registry proposed host pay.odelhub.com is configuration only — not evidence of public deployment.
